About

A Student of
Systems

I am Kartik Kashyap — a pre-final year B.Tech IT student who refuses to stay in one domain. Every system I build is an attempt to understand something deeper: about crime, about behaviour, about how we learn, about how societies fracture and cohere.

I work at the intersection of AI and human complexity — building forensic platforms that must hold up in court, simulators that teach by making you feel the consequences, and tools that don't just process data but respect the weight of what data represents.

When I'm not engineering systems, I'm writing scripts, studying game theory, animating mathematical ideas, or building interactive stories that make people look at ordinary things differently. A pen. A supply chain. A crime statistic. A philosophical tension.

The goal is always the same: make the invisible, visible.
